A* from the ground up

How A* finds a route

1. Model a graph

Nodes are possible positions. Edges are allowed moves, and every edge has a cost.

2. Score candidates

g is cost so far, h estimates what remains, and f = g + h.

3. Explore wisely

A* repeatedly chooses the open node with the smallest f score and tests its neighbors.

4. Rebuild the answer

When the goal is reached, stored parent links reveal the lowest-cost route.
